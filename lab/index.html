About

The Fast and the Furious: Tokyo Drift (PS2, 2006) running in your browser. It is not an emulator: the game's PS2 code was statically recompiled to WebAssembly and its graphics are drawn with WebGPU. You bring your own disc image; it stays on your device.

Changes from the original

  • Widescreen up to 32:9
  • Higher render resolution, edge smoothing, high refresh rate screens
  • Camera shake & sway removable (one switch: bumps, crashes, gear changes, nitrous, corner sway, g-force lean)
  • Optional glow, touch controls for phones, any controller with analog triggers and rumble
  • Saves in your browser, encrypted backup files you can keep anywhere
